Delete your Duudle account

You can delete your account and its data at any time, right in the app.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Duudle and tap Me in the bottom bar.
  2. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  3. Tap Delete account again to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted straight away. This can't be undone.

What gets deleted

  • Your profile, username and character
  • Your friends and friend requests
  • Doodles and flash cards you sent and received
  • Your chats with friends (your friends lose them too)
  • Saved doodles, mutes, blocks, notification settings and your Terms acceptance
  • Everything Duudle kept on your phone for the account

If someone reported a doodle or chat involving you, or you reported one, that doodle or chat is kept until we've reviewed the report, then deleted. Report records are kept for safety reviews.

Don't have the app any more?

Email [contact email coming soon] with the subject "Delete my Duudle account" and tell us your username. Because Duudle accounts don't use a password or email, we'll check that the account isn't in use on any phone before deleting it, and reply when it's done, within 30 days.
