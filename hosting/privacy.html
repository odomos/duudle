Privacy Policy

Last updated 2 November 2026. Duudle is made by Arnav Chauhan, an independent developer. Contact: doodlebyarnav425@gmail.com.

What Duudle collects

  • Account: a random account ID, the username you pick and your character. If you link Google to recover your account, your Google email address and name are kept with your account.
  • What you send: doodles (the lines you draw), flash cards you pick, chat messages and chat themes. Friends you send them to can see and keep them.
  • Your connections: friends, friend requests, and the people you mute or block.
  • Reports: when you report someone, the report (reason, what it's about and anything you write) and the doodle or chat it concerns.
  • Device data: a notification token so doodles reach your phone, counters that limit how much one account can send per hour or day, and a check that requests come from the real app (Google Play Integrity / Firebase App Check).
  • Crash reports: when the app crashes or a pop-up fails, a technical report (what went wrong in the code, phone model, Android and app version, a random installation ID). Crash reports never contain your drawings or messages.

Duudle has no ads, no advertising ID and no analytics, and doesn't read your contacts, photos or location.

Why

  • To run the app: deliver doodles and messages to your friends and show them as pop-ups.
  • To keep people safe: enforce the Terms, review reports, and stop spam and abuse.
  • To fix problems: crash reports show what to repair.

We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who handles it

Duudle runs on Google Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics, App Check). Google processes the data for us under its own terms. It's stored on Google servers in the United States and encrypted in transit.

Your friends see your username, your character and what you send them. We share data with others only if the law requires it, or to report content that exploits children to the authorities.

How long it's kept

  • Your account, doodles, messages and connections: until you delete them or your account.
  • Reports: report records are kept for up to 1 year after we've reviewed them. A doodle or chat under an open report is kept until the review is done, even if an account is deleted.
  • Crash reports: up to 90 days.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app, Me > Account > Delete account. Without the app, see how to request deletion.
  • Pop-ups: turn off "Display over other apps", mute a friend or snooze pop-ups at any time. Notifications can be turned off in Android settings.
  • Questions or a copy of your data: email us and we'll reply within 30 days.

Children

Duudle is for people 13 and older. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll update the date above and tell you in the app.
