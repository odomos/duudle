# Everyday

- **Pack id:** `everyday`
- **Stickers:** `card10` to `card17` (8): hello, laughing, winking, shocked, sleepy, heart-eyes,
  grumpy, crying. Search keywords are in `FlashCards.kt`.
- **Art:** Android VectorDrawables, `app/src/main/res/drawable/sticker_everyday_*.xml`.
- **Moves:** hello bounces, laughing wobbles, heart-eyes pulses; the rest stay still.

Source: Original artwork drawn for Duudle by Claude (an AI assistant) at the owner's request on
2026-11-15, in the style of the Home screen characters (flat pastel shapes with simple faces).
Not copied or traced from any existing work.
