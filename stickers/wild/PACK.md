# Wild Bunch

- **Pack id:** `wild`
- **Stickers:** `card82 to card93` (12): Roar, Boss mode, Game on, Chillin', Big hug, Say what, Ooh ooh, Who me, Nap time, Cuddle, Sneaky, Snack time. Search keywords are in `StickerPacks.kt`.
- **Art:** Android VectorDrawables, `app/src/main/res/drawable/sticker_wild_*.xml`. Each sticker is an
  animal character (lion, tiger, elephant, monkey, koala or raccoon) with the words drawn in as outlines, so it stays sharp at any size.
  The label says what the sticker says, for TalkBack.
- **Moves:** Roar, Game on, Big hug, Ooh ooh, Cuddle; the rest stay still.

Source: Original artwork drawn for Duudle by Claude (an AI assistant) at the owner's request on
2026-11-17, in the style of the earlier packs (flat pastel shapes, simple faces). The animals and the
small props are original designs and no existing character was copied or traced. The owner asked for
more sticker packs with animals.

Lettering: Fredoka Bold, copyright The Fredoka Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`stickers/FONT-LICENSE-Fredoka-OFL.txt`). The letters were converted to vector outlines; the font file
itself is not in the app.
