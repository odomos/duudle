# Dogs

- **Pack id:** `dogs`
- **Stickers:** `card26` to `card33` (8): panting, goofy with crossed eyes, head tilt, begging
  puppy eyes, heart eyes, growling, excited with a tennis ball, sleeping. Search keywords are in
  `FlashCards.kt`.
- **Art:** Android VectorDrawables, `app/src/main/res/drawable/sticker_dogs_*.xml`.
- **Moves:** panting bounces, goofy wobbles, begging puppy pulses; the rest stay still.

Source: Original artwork drawn for Duudle by Claude (an AI assistant) at the owner's request on
2026-11-17, in the style of the Everyday and Cats packs (flat pastel shapes, simple faces). The dogs
are original designs: no existing character was copied (deliberately no blue dog, no white dog with
black ears, no eye patch), and nothing was traced from any image.
