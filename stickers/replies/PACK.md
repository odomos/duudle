# Replies

- **Pack id:** `replies`
- **Stickers:** `card46 to card57` (12): Yes, No, OK, Maybe, Thanks, Please, Sorry, No worries, Sure, Same, Really, Idk. Search keywords are in `StickerPacks.kt`.
- **Art:** Android VectorDrawables, `app/src/main/res/drawable/sticker_replies_*.xml`. Each sticker is an
  animal character (cat, dog, bunny, bear, panda, fox, pig, chick or penguin) with the words drawn in
  as outlines, so it stays sharp at any size. The label says what the sticker says, for TalkBack.
- **Moves:** Yes, Thanks and Please move; the rest stay still.

Source: Original artwork drawn for Duudle by Claude (an AI assistant) at the owner's request on
2026-11-17, in the style of the Everyday, Cats and Dogs packs (flat pastel shapes, simple faces). The
animals are original designs and no existing character was copied or traced. The owner asked for
stickers that cover what people say in a short conversation, with normal text on animal characters.

Lettering: Fredoka Bold, copyright The Fredoka Project Authors, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`stickers/FONT-LICENSE-Fredoka-OFL.txt`). The letters were converted to vector outlines; the font file
itself is not in the app.

Licence: made for the Duudle project (owner: Arnav Chauhan).

Checked: no third-party characters, logos or watermarks. The only text is the sticker's own words.
