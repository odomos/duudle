# Cats

- **Pack id:** `cats`
- **Stickers:** `card18` to `card25` (8): toothy grin, laughing until it cries, shocked, smug,
  napping, heart eyes, hungry, hissing. Search keywords are in `FlashCards.kt`.
- **Art:** Android VectorDrawables, `app/src/main/res/drawable/sticker_cats_*.xml`.
- **Moves:** grin bounces, laughing wobbles, heart-eyes pulses; the rest stay still.

Source: Original artwork drawn for Duudle by Claude (an AI assistant) at the owner's request on
2026-11-17, in the style of the Everyday pack (flat pastel shapes, simple faces). The owner asked
for funny, exaggerated reaction faces like a set of cartoon-cat images they had seen; only the
idea of expressive cat reactions was used. The cats are original designs: no existing character,
colours or features were copied (deliberately no blue or olive-green cats and no big red noses),
and nothing was traced from any image.
